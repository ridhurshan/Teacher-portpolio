About Sir

Twenty-one years of
turning “I can’t” into “I got it”.

Nuwan Perera has taught mathematics in the Negombo area since 2004 — first as a government school teacher at Maris Stella College, and since 2011 full-time at his own institute.

His classes are built on one idea: no student moves on until the step in front of them is clear. Every lesson ends with a worked past-paper question, every month ends with a marked test, and every parent gets the results by SMS.
